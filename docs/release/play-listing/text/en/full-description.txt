Flute practice that feels like play! 🎶

Flute Assistant is a cheerful practice buddy for children who are learning the flute. Big colourful buttons, friendly animals, yummy sweets and almost nothing to read, so kids find their own way around. Four little helpers live inside:

🎼 FLUTE: MAKE UP YOUR OWN TUNES
Tap or hold the colourful keys and your tune appears on a real music staff, note by note. Hold a key longer for a longer note. Press Play to hear it, then press "Listen to me" and play it on your own flute: the app listens and marks every note you get right. Wrong note? It shows you the fingering, so you can try again. Keep six tunes in six little books: a lollipop, a cookie, a cupcake, a donut, an ice cream and a candy.

🥁 METRONOME: KEEP THE BEAT WITH ANIMALS
Slow as a snail or fast as a cheetah? Drag the animal around the ring to pick a speed and watch it hop on every beat. The click keeps going while you read your sheet music in another app.

🎯 TUNER: BRING YOUR NOTE HOME
Play a note and watch it float above or below its home ring. Arrows and friendly tips like "A little higher" or "Faster air" help you bring it home for a big "Perfect!". Not sure how a note goes? Pick it to hear it and see its fingering.

🎵 NOTES: TWO GAMES FOR READING MUSIC
Find: a note name pops up, and you slide your finger to its place on the staff.
Hear: the app plays a few notes, and you find them on the staff by ear.
Every note you learn fills its key with colour, and a full key wins a star. Collect flames for notes in a row and a trophy at the end of each round.
